Forensic Concept: The Cryptographic Avalanche Effect

Why SHA-256 is the gold standard in digital forensics court cases:

• Strict Admissibility (FRE Rule 901/902): Digital evidence cannot be accepted in court unless an investigator can prove it has remained completely unmodified from the crime scene to the courtroom.

• Avalanche Effect: In SHA-256, changing even a single bit in a file causes more than 50% of the output hash characters to flip unpredictably. This makes silent evidence tampering computationally impossible to hide.
